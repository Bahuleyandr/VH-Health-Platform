# Clinical Service Continuity Activation-Readiness Tracker

**Authority snapshot:** `github/main` at
`2d15628eeaa891404285fbddec512e89b594487c` (re-fetched 2026-08-02).

**Purpose:** this is the single working paper for what still separates the
repository state from live continuity capabilities, including the C-D11
activation-cohort sitting. It consolidates existing gates; it does not create,
approve, close, waive, deploy, or activate any gate.

**State key:** `MERGED-INERT` means the relevant substrate is on `main` but is
default-off, held, unselected, suspended, or otherwise unable to activate by
merge alone. `OPERATOR`, `OWNER-DECISION`, `HARDWARE`, and `EXTERNAL` identify
the authority or dependency that must supply the named input or evidence.
`OPEN-QUESTION` is used when a required gate is verifiable but its completion
or one of those five state classifications is not verifiable from the
authoritative records. “Satisfied as decision input” means only that the cited
decision is countersigned; downstream publication, provisioning, drill, and
activation gates remain open. The owning-authority column names who supplies
or accepts the evidence, not a new assignment by this tracker.

the prior source in history, add the new source inline, and never interpret a
merge, CI pass, render, fixture, or repository placeholder as live activation.
